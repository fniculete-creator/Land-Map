SB 1123 / SB 684 screening preset
  • Vacant lot (no/negligible improvements)
  • Single-family zone & lot ≤ 1.5 acres, or multifamily zone & lot ≤ 5 acres
  • Outside High / Very High fire hazard zones
  • Coastal parcels stay eligible but lose ministerial streamlining (Coastal Development Permit required) — shown with a warning.
  • Hillside parcels flagged — extra development constraints.

Screening heuristic only — verify every lot in ZIMAS and with counsel.
